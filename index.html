Store policy

Gift wrapping: the store gift-wraps any order for 12 riyals, with a handwritten card.

Returns: unused items can be returned within 14 days of delivery with the original receipt. Refunds go back to the original payment method within five working days.

Opening hours: the customer service team answers messages from Sunday to Thursday, 9 in the morning until 6 in the evening, Riyadh time.
